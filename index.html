GigsForMe is hiring a UI/UX Designer Intern!

Mandatory Application Method: Filling out our official Google Form is mandatory to apply for this internship. Only applications submitted through the official Google Form will be considered. Applications received through Unstop Quick Apply or any other method will not be considered.

Apply Here: Click Here

About the Company:

  • GigsForMe is an AI-powered freelancing marketplace focused on connecting talented freelancers, clients, and businesses through a modern, transparent, and technology-driven platform. We are building a next-generation ecosystem that simplifies hiring, project collaboration, and talent discovery.
  • As an early-stage startup, you'll have the opportunity to work directly with the Founder and product team, contributing to real features.

Responsibilities

  • Support intuitive, accessible, and visually engaging user experiences across web and mobile.
  • Conduct basic user research, review feedback, and translate insights into user flows, wireframes, and polished interface concepts.
  • Create prototypes, design components, and interaction details while collaborating with product and development teams.
  • Participate in design reviews and iterate based on stakeholder and user feedback.

Requirements

  • Applicants with 0–1 year of experience in UI/UX, product design, visual design, or a related area are welcome.
  • Familiarity with common design principles, wireframes, prototypes, and responsive design is helpful.
  • Good communication, attention to detail, and willingness to learn in a collaborative environment.

Internship details

  • Duration: 3 months
  • Work mode: Work from Home
  • Compensation: Unpaid
